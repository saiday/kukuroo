Kukuroo is a free Cloudflare Worker plus KV. Deploy it once; there is no host, no TLS to renew, no process to babysit at 3am.

No company in the middle.

Your keys live in your Worker, and the message is encrypted to your device. Nobody's dashboard ever sees your alerts.

No app to build.

Your phone has rendered these natively since iOS 18.4. No App Store, no $99 a year for four words on a lock screen.

The whole integration

# from cron, CI, a Raspberry Pi, your backend, a shell alias curl -X POST https://push.example.com/push/send \ -H "authorization: Bearer $KUKUROO_SEND_TOKEN" \ -d '{"notification":{"title":"Backup finished", "body":"nightly to B2, 2.3 GB in 41s", "navigate":"https://push.example.com/"}}' {"delivered":1,"removed":0,"failures":[]}

That is not the quick start. That is the entire integration: anything that can make an HTTP call can ring your phone.

Install once, in three moves

Deploy your Worker

Copy the template, generate your keys with one command, deploy. Storage provisions itself.

npm install kukuroo cp -r node_modules/kukuroo/templates/standalone my-kukuroo cd my-kukuroo && npm install npx kukuroo init npx wrangler deploy
Put it on your Home Screen

Open your new page in Safari, Add to Home Screen, type the invite code. Thirty seconds, once per device. That is Apple's one rule, and the only manual step in the whole thing.

POST when something happens

Drop the curl above into whatever should be able to reach you. The response tells you exactly how many devices it reached.

The fine print

Told straight: receiving needs Safari, iOS 18.4 or later on a phone, and one send reaches about 50 devices on the free plan. Every limit and sharp edge is written down plainly in the README, before you deploy anything, because that is where trust comes from.
